## Brand & Style

This design system is conceived as a digital sanctuary and high-curation gallery platform for the painter Alejandro Fernández Tellería. Rooted in the psychological gravity of Goya's *Pinturas Negras*, visceral figurative expressionism, and tactile materiality, the interface behaves like an unlit museum vault where light reveals form and impasto textures.

The aesthetic fuses **Editorial Minimalism** with **Tenebrist Brutalism**:
- **Atmosphere:** Deep psychological weight, contemplative stillness, and theatrical chiaroscuro.
- **Target Audience:** Contemporary art collectors, museum curators, auction houses, critics, and international gallery directors who value physical weight, textural integrity, and uncompromised artistic voice over commercial polish.
- **Emotional Response:** Somber reverence, intellectual curiosity, intimate discomfort, and unhurried observation. The design refuses decorative noise; every interface element yields absolute prominence to the raw, flesh-like paint handling and brooding canvas surfaces.

## Colors

The palette operates under strict chiaroscuro discipline, mirroring raw pigment grounds, burnt sienna, animal bone, and dried carmine:

- **Neutral / Deep Canvas (`#0e0e10` and `#060607`):** The primary canvas background acts as an endless, light-absorbing bitumen void. It eliminates digital backlight glare and replicates the muted velvet of contemporary exhibition spaces.
- **Tertiary / Bone Chalk (`#e5e1db`):** The primary editorial text and structure color. It possesses the organic warmth of aged rag paper, raw gesso, and calcium white, avoiding harsh digital `#ffffff`.
- **Primary / Weathered Sienna (`#c48b3c`):** An oxidized, mineral-laden gold and raw earth tone. Reserved for active cursor points, archive indices, exhibition years, and critical accents.
- **Secondary / Visceral Carmine (`#8f2222`):** A bruised, clotted madder red. Used sparingly for curated warnings, sale acquisitions, provenance seals, and micro-hover states that demand immediate visceral focus.
- **Subtle Substrates:**
  - Border and Dividers: `rgba(229, 225, 219, 0.08)` to `rgba(229, 225, 219, 0.16)`.
  - Secondary Text / Museum Metadata: `#858380` (diluted bone dust).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pits the monumental theatricality of Italian high-contrast serifs against clinical archival precision:

- **Bodoni Moda (Display & Headings):** Theatrical high-contrast modern serif echoing 18th/19th-century academic monographs and historic gallery catalogues. It introduces extreme stroke modulation—paper-thin hairlines contrasting heavy vertical stems. It commands exhibition titling and series names.
- **Newsreader (Editorial & Body):** An expressive, literary serif built for long-form critical essays, manifestos, and curatorial statements. Its humanist proportions ensure comfortable immersion in low light.
- **Geist (Cataloguing & Technical Index):** A monolinear, razor-sharp technical sans used exclusively for artwork identification numbers, mediums, dimensions, years, and museum ledger coordinates. All technical labels are styled in small uppercase tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.08em`) to mirror archival exhibition wall tags.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial architecture abandons symmetrical, predictable web blocks in favor of **asymmetric biennial-curated pacing**:

- **Grid Architecture:** A bespoke 12-column fluid grid governed by variable column spans (e.g., column offsets like `col-span-7 col-start-2` juxtaposed with narrow caption corridors of `col-span-3 col-start-10`).
- **Dramatic Negative Space:** Large voids of `#0e0e10` are deliberately preserved around single masterworks, creating visual isolation akin to spotlighted gallery displays. Vertical flow relies on wide, breathable rhythm with section gaps reaching up to `space-xl` (4rem to 8rem).
- **Responsive Adaptations:**
  - **Desktop (1280px+):** Full asymmetric offset galleries, decoupled image-caption pairs, and horizontal scroll trajectories for archival portfolios.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout; gutter compresses to `1.5rem`; offset margins collapse into controlled 1-column step stagger.
  - **Mobile (<768px):** Stacks down to a single-column monolithic column with strict `1.5rem` canvas edge margins, preserving generous top/bottom padding per piece to protect isolation.

## Elevation & Depth

In keeping with the tenebrist aesthetic, elevation relies on **pure tonal gradation and light containment**, avoiding glossy blurs or standard dropshadows:

- **Flat Tonal Grounding:** Elevated planes emerge through discrete stepped value shifts from ground charcoal (`#0e0e10`) to asphalt slate (`#141417`) and oxidized carbon (`#1b1b20`).
- **Spectral Light Vignetting:** Artwork framing incorporates ambient, non-directional chiaroscuro: an ultra-subtle radial falloff (`radial-gradient(ellipse at center, rgba(196, 139, 60, 0.04) 0%, transparent 70%)`) that mimics single-source museum illumination hitting raw linen canvas.
- **Low-Contrast Razor Outlines:** Modal panes, viewer overlays, and zoomed details are bounded by hairline 1px borders of `rgba(229, 225, 219, 0.12)`. No floating card drop shadows are permitted; elements are either carved directly out of the dark or framed by hairline divisions.

## Shapes

This design system adheres to absolute geometric severity with a **roundedness factor of 0 (Sharp)**:

- **0px Radius Everywhere:** Every button, card container, interactive thumbnail, image frame, modal window, and input field features absolute 90-degree corners.
- **Conceptual Intent:** Curves in this universe belong exclusively to the human anatomy, distorted faces, and heavy brushwork inside Alejandro Fernández Tellería's paintings. The interface serves purely as the stretcher bar, frame, and architectural wall—cold, structural, and unyielding.

## Components

### Buttons & Curatorial Triggers
- **Primary Action (Acquisition / Dossier):** Solid `#e5e1db` background, `#060607` sharp typography in `Geist` technical caps. Hover state reverses into `#c48b3c` with a 200ms ease-out transition.
- **Ghost Action (Inspect / Audio Guide / Archive):** Transparent background, 1px perimeter in `rgba(229, 225, 219, 0.25)`, text in bone chalk. Hover introduces visceral carmine border (`#8f2222`) and faint `#8f2222` text tint.
- **Dimensions:** Strict `44px` height baseline with `space-md` (1rem) horizontal padding.

### Ficha Técnica (Artwork Detail & Metadata Cards)
- Borderless structural block docked alongside or beneath the canvas viewport.
- Headings set in `Bodoni Moda` (`headline-sm`), followed by an uncompromising horizontal rule (`1px solid rgba(229, 225, 219, 0.1)`).
- Metadata list laid out as tabular pairs: Property names (`TÉCNICA`, `DIMENSIONES`, `AÑO`, `ESTADO`) in `Geist` `0.6875rem` uppercase at 40% opacity, values rendered in `Newsreader` `0.9375rem` italic bone chalk.

### Artwork Cards & Exhibition Tiles
- Zero border radius, framing the painting edge-to-edge.
- Hover interaction: The image transitions from 88% brightness to 100% luminosity, accompanied by an imperceptible 1.01x zoom scale. A floating indicator pill in `Geist` caps (`INSPECCIONAR LIENZO`) fades into the lower corner.

### Form Inputs & Acquisition Inquiries
- Single underline border (`1px solid rgba(229, 225, 219, 0.2)`) on dark void; no enclosing rectangular box.
- Background remains transparent `#00000000`.
- Focus state transforms the bottom border into raw sienna (`#c48b3c`) accompanied by floating technical labels in `Geist`.

### Selection Controls & Filters (Medium, Series, Year)
- Checkboxes and radio selectors replaced by minimal bracketed indicators (`[ + ]`, `[ — ]`) or typographic toggle markers in Geist uppercase. Selected tags receive a subtle sienna baseline underline.
